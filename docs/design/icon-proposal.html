Semantic diff icons: today vs suggested

Pierre's semantic view marks each kind of change with a gutter icon. Pierre's current icons are on the left, our suggestion is on the right, and the reasons follow. The colors are unchanged.

What the research found

  • Two pairs of change types can't be told apart today. Edited and Renamed share one icon and one color, and so do Moved and Reordered. Showing a difference by color alone fails WCAG 1.4.1, and here even the color is the same.
  • The extract and inline arrows are the “external link” icon. Lucide and Codicons use exactly that shape to mean “open link”.
  • » already has a meaning in diffs. Phabricator uses it for indentation-only changes.
  • Tools that link the two ends of a move use words. Gerrit shows “Moved to lines 2–4” and “Moved from lines 4–6”. SemanticDiff draws a connector and labels the block “Moved without changes”.
  • Icons need labels. Nielsen Norman Group: “a text label must be present alongside an icon”, and don't rely on hover to show it.

Principles behind the suggestion

  1. Each change type gets its own shape; color comes on top of that.
  2. For a move, the shape on each side says “leaves” or “arrives”. Extract and inline already differ in shape from each other, so there the label gives the direction.
  3. A short visible label names the other end, either a line number or a function name, and is clickable where possible. This is what fixes the ambiguity; the icon only helps you scan.
  4. Use well-known icons from one set, Lucide (ISC license), so the stroke style is consistent.
